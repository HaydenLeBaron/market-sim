Intuitively Marginal Utility is the rate of change of utility as you consume one more unit of a good.

What is with the Marginal Utility of a good xi (MU_xi) in an n-dimensional space?

/** The marginal utility of good xi is the partial derivative of the utility function with respect to good xi*/
let MU_xi(x1, x2, x3, ..., xn) = ∂U(x1, x2, x3, ..., xn) / ∂xi
  where i ∈ {1, 2, 3, ..., n}

What, then is the Marginal Rate of Substitution of a good xi for a good xj (MRS_xi_xj) in an n-dimensional space?

/** The marginal rate of substitution of good xi for good xj is the ratio of the marginal utilities of good xi and good xj */
let MRS_xi_xj(x1, x2, x3, ..., xn) = MU_xi(x1, x2, x3, ..., xn) / MU_xj(x1, x2, x3, ...xn)
  where i, j ∈ {1, 2, 3, ..., n}
